# Adult FotH — For a Church Prepared for Revival

*The original adult design. Not yet walked; being retrofitted with what the CCA pilot and the [FotH Evening](../evening/index.md) have taught.*

**The adult sequence: three series, walked leadership-first.**

A church that says *we are interested in trying this out* is not handed a program. It is offered a challenge: your leaders walk the year first. First for ourselves, then for our teens. The three series here — **Getting Started**, **Going Deeper**, and **Going Out** — are the original adult design of the Fellowship of the Heart work, being retrofitted with everything the twenty-two-week school-year edition taught us: the container as first curriculum, the feedback round, the practice holds, and the breaks that reveal what the room has actually kept.

A leadership team that completes this year is formed to lead the family edition, and has proven it wants to. That is the whole gate, and it is offered plainly.
