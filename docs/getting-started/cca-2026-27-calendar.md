# How to read this page

CCA clubs run twenty-two weeks across four quarters — 6/5/6/5, Wednesdays, beginning September 2, 2026. The Getting Started curriculum is fifteen sessions. This page lays the fifteen onto the twenty-two **without renumbering anything**: every lesson plan keeps its "Week N" identity, and this overlay maps each club *meeting* to its session. The seven extra meetings have their own names and their own jobs. A future body running the series straight through on fifteen consecutive weeks needs only the curriculum; this page is one household's calendar.

All sessions run per the v1.3 pilot edition: high-school teens and parents in family clusters, parents first, and the Leader Feedback Round closing every meeting.

# What the seven extras are for

Three jobs, deliberately assigned:

1. **Re-entry (Meetings 12, 18, and the Meeting-7 opening block).** Every break is a natural test of whether the practices survived without the container holding them. The re-entries rebuild the room — and quietly answer the only question a calm survey never can: what held under load?
2. **More runnings (Meetings 14 and 19).** The fifteen-session map had four teen-led slots — enough to form one Companion-in-Formation. The overlay adds reps so that *every* willing candidate receives, assists, and leads — and the wider every-teen rotation (v1.3) has room to breathe.
3. **Warmth and slack (Meetings 16, 17, 20).** A family night that is simply a family night, one float for the calendar's surprises, and a consolidation evening before the two commissionings — so the year ends gathered, not sprinted.

# Measurement touchpoints (summary — the full plan is Handbook Section 8)

- **Weekly:** the Three Vital Signs log at every team debrief; the Leader Feedback Round noted (a scheduled weekly instance of costly telling).
- **Quarterly:** the Pulse at Meetings 6, 11, and 16; the Post-Series Survey at Meeting 20.
- **At every re-entry:** rhythm-card survival, Path Home Card uses, time-to-settled-container — the three break experiments.
- **Outside reads:** invited Walk-In Readings under the covering's authority (vetted reader, blind to content, private report), once or twice a quarter at ordinary meetings — never at ceremonies.

# The two gates this calendar watches

1. **Student leadership in writing** (CCA legal review, in motion): needed before Meeting 4 (Sep 23) for the full teen-led Story II — adult-led with teen assisting until then; the series runs either way.
2. **Commissioning at year's end** (Meeting 22): the FC1 exit gate per Handbook Section 11 — a sending witnessed by the whole cohort, with each commissioned Companion's covering named and, for a graduating senior, the referral card built for the campus they are actually headed to.
