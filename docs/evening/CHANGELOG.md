# Change Log

*FotH Evening — Two-Week Rhythm · Teen-Led*

## v2 — September 16, 2026 — the first evening's debrief (Session 5, run 9/15)

The first evening ran Tuesday, September 15, as Session 5 — the four
prototype teens plus parents, no newcomers, which turned into a blessing:
full time on one session, real teen-leader training, and a live curriculum
scrub. The teens handed the leadership back and forth all evening. Content
landed: the friendship/connecting focus tangibly resonated. Timing held —
finished ~10 minutes early with six; a split-and-merge would have cost ~5.

Changes from the debrief, applied to Session 5 (page, deck, run sheet,
take-home) — items marked *(series)* ripple to every session and land in
the ripple pass:

- **The Four Connects grounding** *(series)*: every session now opens on
  the map — a new grounding slide (the four-boxes image, used consistently
  series-wide) locating the session on Self → Others → God → Mission; the
  four conditions of friendship are how *connecting with others* deepens
  into *loving others* (Matt. 22:37–40). Handbook and session page updated.
- **Four Connects as on-ramp** *(program, recorded)*: conviction from the
  evening — run the Four Connects as an on-ramp inviting people to taste
  the heart work before committing to an extended series. Design to follow;
  not yet in the program docs.
- **Split only if the room needs it** *(series)*: split-and-merge blocks
  annotated (speaker notes + run sheet) as necessary only at size; a small
  room stays one circle or drops into quick pair work in place — also the
  standing time-recovery lever (~5 minutes saved vs a full split).
- **Merge slide clarified**: the most-common/rarest ask now two explicit
  sub-bullets (most often present across mapped friendships / most often
  missing).
- **"Homework practice" vocabulary** *(series)*: "the practice" renamed
  "the homework practice" wherever the take-home practice is meant — deck,
  page, run sheet, take-home sheet, handbook.
- **The small-number frame**: new teaching beat + slide bullet — deep
  investment in a small number of friendships, starting with one; two or
  three may be all a life can hold at this depth. Tonight: one friendship,
  one condition.
- **A pair leads, visibly** *(series)*: welcome names the leading pair and
  the handoffs as design; the pair practices the session together
  beforehand (run sheet + handbook + speaker notes).
- **Container holder = the busier seat** *(series)*: handbook and run sheet
  record the pre-session training conversation — clock plus a continuous
  read against safe/present/clear/intentional, spotting the triggered or
  checked-out, weight handed to the backstop adult.
- **Leader Notes handout** *(new artifact, prototype)*: the deck's speaker
  notes as a printed in-hand script for the leading pair (Session 5 built;
  per-session builds follow if it proves out).
- **The take-home on screen** *(series)*: the commissioning block now shows
  the *actual* take-home sheet — two slides (the daily rhythm / the four
  parts), replacing the school-form H5.6 card image, so the screen matches
  the sheet in the room's hands.
- **"More than the four"** — new two-minute wrap-up at the merge's tail
  (content presenter): the four conditions are foundational — what makes a
  close friendship possible — but a good friend also gives you energy,
  leaves you uplifted, is warm and caring, maybe even funny; and these are
  a way to choose *which* friendships to deepen with this process and
  intentionality. New slide + page + run sheet.
- **Check-in wording aligned**: "midweek" → "end of week one" on the
  practice slide, matching sheet and handbook.
- **Clock fix**: bridge-slide notes' stray "back at 5:11" (school-form
  clock) corrected to 8:08.
- **John's review round (9/16)**: teaching slide retitled *Friendship
  Mapping with Four Conditions*; cluster language settled to **pairs**
  throughout the deck (bridge slide + notes) for timing; the small-number
  line demoted to a sub-bullet under "pick one friendship"; quotes dropped
  from the choice line on *More Than the Four*; the three inserted slides
  given interim corner numbers (59a, 66a, 68a) — the full master renumber
  lands with the series rebuild at the ripple.
