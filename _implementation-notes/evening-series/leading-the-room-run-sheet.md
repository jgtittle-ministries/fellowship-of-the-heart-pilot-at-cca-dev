# Leading the Room — A Facilitation Evening

*For the leads: the teen leads, and the parents who serve as safety adults. Not one of the numbered evenings (added 17 November 2026). John leads; the teen leads take part in it first.*

**Aim.** The room owns the evening. Each lead practices the four moves that hand a room its own evening: ask an open question, wait, turn it to the room, lean back.

**Anchor.** 1 Corinthians 14:26; James 1:19; 1 Peter 4:10.

## The run sheet (7:00–8:30)

## The practice moments

**Announce them first.** Before the rounds, John says aloud that two parents will play moments, so no one mistakes practice for the real thing.

**Something heavy.** A parent, in role: "Honestly, I've been really low lately, worse than I've let anyone see." The move: thank them, don't dig, hand it to the safety adult at once.

**Something that isn't right.** A parent, in role: "I heard God tell me I don't need to read the Bible anymore." The move: "We hear that," and on. Correction comes afterward, privately, by a parent or the safety adult.

## Watch-fors

**No grading.** Nobody is scored or compared. A round that goes badly is the evening working.

**If something real surfaces,** stop the practice. The safety adult takes it, as on any evening.

**The question set before the evening.** In the next two evenings, does the talk run from member to member more often? The 7:05 drawing is the starting point; the answer goes in John's debrief notes, with nothing kept on any one person.
