# Teen Lead Sign-Up — Fall Quarter 2026

**Fellowship of the Heart, evening series at the Tittle home.** Dinner at
6:00, session 7:00-8:30, done by 9:00. Monday or Tuesday of each meeting week, set
with the families.

Two leads each evening. The **container holder** opens and closes, holds the
circles, and watches the room: safety and warmth, presence, clear and
intentional. The **content presenter** carries the teaching and the
practice, and commissions the homework. Sign up as ready and willing; the
roles rotate, and the aim is for every teen to try both roles across the
quarter. The leading team walks the session with John ahead of time, at the
previous meeting's dinner or by phone in the off week.
