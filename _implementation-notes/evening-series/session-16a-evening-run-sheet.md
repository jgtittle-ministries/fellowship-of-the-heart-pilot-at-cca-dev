# Evening 16 — CARRYING THE WORD

*The end of the third movement, Mission and Rhythm. An evening-form session with no school-series counterpart (added 5 November 2026). The rhythm has been built; tonight each family takes the most portable thing there is to carry, a passage learned by heart, together.*

**Aim.** Each family chooses a short passage, begins to learn it by heart together, says it to the room, and takes it home to be said every day.

**Anchor.** Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Psalm 119:11; Colossians 3:16.

## What this evening asks of the room

**The teens teach.** The five ways to carry a passage are taught by the teens, each taking one, and the room tries each on the same verse. The Carrying Card holds them for the tables and the fridge.

**Parents go first.** In each family, a parent says the passage first, stumbles included. Teens learn to carry the Word by watching their parents carry it.

**One practice verse for the room.** Psalm 119:11 is short and says what tonight is for. Everyone learns it together before the families turn to their own.

## The homework handout

The Evening 16 sheet: the family's passage, the part each carries and the daily moment at the top; week one, the passage said together every day; the end-of-week-one check-in, partners saying their part to each other; week two, one journal line, where this passage met me this week; and the passage brought back to The Float.

## Watch-fors

**No contests, no grading, no shame.** Nobody is scored, and nobody is compared. Forgetting is part of learning; a family that gets one line is doing the practice.

**Every age gets a length it can carry.** Watch the youngest and the ones who find memorizing hard. Shorten their portion quietly and without fuss.

**It is the Word, not a performance.** Say it slowly, meaning it. The goal is a passage on the heart, not a passage recited fast.

**A family without a passage yet.** Offer Psalm 119:11, the room's verse. It is enough to start; they may choose another during the week.
