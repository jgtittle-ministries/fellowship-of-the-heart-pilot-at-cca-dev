# Evening 9 — HEART BIBLE STUDY

*The first evening back after the Christmas break, and the last of the second movement, Connect with God. An evening-form session with no school-series counterpart (added 5 November 2026). The protocol behind it is IJH Vol 5, A28 Heart Bible Study with PROAPT.*

**Aim.** Connect with God: PROAPT in its group form. The room hears God together in one passage, and a teen leads the room's hearing (Heb 5:12).

**Anchor.** 1 Samuel 3:1–10; Nehemiah 8:8; Acts 13:1–3.

## What this evening asks of the room

**Nothing new on the first night back.** Every step is one the room already knows: PROAPT from Evening 6, the short form of Any Doubts? from Evening 8, the Tell from every evening. Tonight puts them together.

**The welcome is short on purpose.** One sentence each. The full reading of what held over the break is The Return's work, two weeks from now.

**The clock is tight.** The protocol gives a passage sixty to ninety minutes; tonight it has about fifty. If the room runs long, the same-and-different round shortens first. The Tell is never skipped.

**A pair leads, visibly.** The content presenter leads the study with the Reading Card; the container holder keeps the clock, watches the room and leads the Tell. Two named adults hold safety, whoever is leading.

## The homework handout

The Evening 9 sheet, with its companion page "Heart Bible Study, What Each Step Is For": week one, daily PROAPT with tonight's Tell sentence in view; the end-of-week-one check-in; week two, one Heart Bible Study at the family table, a teen leading, on 1 Samuel 3:11–21 or a passage of the family's choosing; and two sentences of preparation for The Return.

## Watch-fors

**No pictures come.** Offer the other door at once: a sound, a gesture, a shape, one word. Never press.

**Commentary creeps in.** Each sharing gets "We hear that" and nothing more. Interpreting someone else's image is the commonest drift.

**An Apply goes heavy.** Honor it in a sentence and move on; it goes to a safety adult after the close. This is a Bible study, not inner work.

**Someone heard a truth they don't yet trust.** Name Any Doubts? as a practice to take home with it. There is no time to run it tonight.
