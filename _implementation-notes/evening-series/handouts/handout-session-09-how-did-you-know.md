# How Did You Know? — Testing What You Hear

*From the Garden evening (Sessions 9–10). Keep it in your journal; we use it
again at Any Doubts?*

---

## 1. Remember a time you really heard

- When and where? What was going on: reading, worship, a conversation,
  outdoors, a hard day?
- What did it look like, sound like, feel like? Write it down and make it fuller.
- What did you hear? What did you do with it, and what happened?
- Can't think of one? A time creation stopped you in your tracks counts.
  God speaks through what he has made (Romans 1:20).

## 2. The checks: how do you know it was God?

| Check | Ask yourself |
|:--|:--|
| **Scripture first** | Does it line up with what God has already said? Scripture is the court; the other checks confirm. |
| **Sense** | Does it make sense, or does it ask me to switch my mind off? |
| **The Spirit's witness** | Is there an inner "yes" that rings true? (Romans 8:16) |
| **Peace** | Is there peace, even if the word is hard? (Colossians 3:15) |
| **Company** | What do people who know me and know God see? |
| **Circumstances** | Do doors open or close around it? |
| **Over time** | What happened when I obeyed? Obeying what I hear opens me to hear more. |

No one check is enough by itself. And wanting something badly can feel like peace.

## 3. On each garden return

After you journal what you noticed, write two lines:
**Does it fit Scripture?** **Who could I tell?**
Notice, don't conclude. If something keeps coming back, test it in company.

## Remember

No one decides for you whether you heard God, and you don't decide for anyone
else. "I'm not sure" is an honest answer. Bring it to Any Doubts?

*"Do not despise prophecies, but test everything; hold fast what is good."
1 Thessalonians 5:20–21 · "My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they
follow me." John 10:27 (ESV)*
