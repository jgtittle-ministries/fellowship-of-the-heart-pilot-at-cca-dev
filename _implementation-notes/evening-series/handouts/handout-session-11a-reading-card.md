# Heart Bible Study — Reading Card

*For the content presenter and the three readers, Evening 9, from 7:25. The passage is 1 Samuel 3:1–10, read from one translation (the ESV), from a physical Bible. Read slowly: two words per second. (pause) = 15 seconds; (silence) = 30. Do not fill the silence.*

---

## Pray (7:25, about 3 minutes)

"Holy Spirit, you are welcome here. Speak. We are listening." Then, in your own words, one or two sentences: we come to hear you, together.

## Three readings (7:28, about 10 minutes)

"Close your eyes if you want. Don't try to work anything out yet. Just let it come to you." (pause)

- **First reader, a teen:** 1 Samuel 3:1–10, all of it, slowly. (silence)
- **Second reader, a parent:** the same passage. (silence)
- **Third reader, a teen:** the same passage, a little slower again. (silence)

"Open your eyes when you're ready."

## Images (7:38, about 10 minutes)

"What pictures, words or feelings came as you listened? One at a time, a sentence or two." A parent starts. Receive each with "We hear that." Nothing more.

*Not a picture person? "A sound, a gesture, a shape, or one word that stayed with you. Any door is a good door."*

## Same and different (7:48, about 7 minutes)

"What did someone else say that you heard too?" Then: "What did you hear that no one else said?" Listen for the thing none of us heard alone, and name it if you see it.

## Observe and Apply (7:55, about 8 minutes)

"Now look at what the passage actually says. Who's in it? Where are they? What happens?" A few answers, short.

"Now the question for each of us: what is the Spirit highlighting in this passage for me, tonight? One specific thing. Write it in your journal." (silence)

"Before you hold on to it, test it: does it agree with Scripture? Does it sound like the Lord, or like accusation?"

## Pray again (8:03, about 4 minutes)

"In silence: tell God what you heard, what you're willing to do, and what you're struggling to be willing to do." (silence) Close with one sentence of prayer.

## The Tell (8:07; the container holder leads)

"One sentence each: what I heard, and what I will do with it." A parent opens. "We hear that." Anyone may pass.

*If something heavy surfaces at any point, honor it in a sentence and carry on; it goes to a safety adult after the close. This is a Bible study, not inner work.*
