# Take-Home Sheet — Evening 9: Heart Bible Study, What Each Step Is For

*A companion to week two's homework practice: one Heart Bible Study at your own table. Keep it open in the middle of the table.*

**What it is.** PROAPT in its group form. PROAPT is how *I* hear God in Scripture; Heart Bible Study is how *we* hear him together. God has often spoken to his people gathered (Nehemiah 8:8; Acts 13:2).

**The one idea to carry.** *Listen for the same and the different.* Each person hears something. The question "what did you hear that someone else heard too, and what did only you hear?" is often where the Spirit shows a group what no one heard alone.

| Step | What the room does | Why it matters | Skip it, and… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pray** | Ask the Spirit to speak, briefly. | We come expecting to *hear*, together. | It becomes a discussion group. |
| **Read, three times** | Three readers, slowly; everyone else with eyes closed; a short silence between readings. | Each reading lands somewhere new. Let the passage come to you before you go to it. | You hear only what you noticed the first time. |
| **Images** | Each says what pictures, words or feelings came as they listened. | The Spirit often speaks in pictures first. Not everyone thinks in pictures: a sound, a gesture or a shape is another door. | The quiet ones never get a way in. |
| **Same and different** | What did someone else hear that you heard too? What did only you hear? | Where the group hears together, and where one person carries a word for the others. | Everyone hears alone, side by side. |
| **Observe** | What the passage actually says: who, what, where, straight off the page. | The ground under everything else. | The images run away from the text. |
| **Apply** | What is the Spirit highlighting for *me*, now? One specific thing. Test it: does it agree with Scripture? | The general word becomes a specific word. | You stay in Bible study, or apply it to someone else. |
| **Pray again** | In silence: what I heard, what I'm willing to do, what I'm struggling to be willing to do. | Hearing becomes a response. | Insight with no answer. |
| **Tell** | One sentence each to the circle: what I heard, and what I will do with it. | Spoken in front of witnesses, the heard word stays. | The most often skipped step, and the one that matters most. |

## Leading it at your table

- **A teen leads; a parent goes first** in each round. Anyone may pass.
- **Receive, don't fix.** Each sharing gets "We hear that." No one comments on another person's hearing.
- **Keep it a Bible study.** If something heavy comes up, honor it briefly and talk about it later, with a parent or a leader.
- **Thirty to forty minutes is plenty** at home, with a short passage.

## When you get stuck

- **No pictures came?** Say a word or phrase that stayed with you. That counts.
- **Not sure it was God?** Tell it anyway, and hold it lightly. What anyone hears is tested, and never binds anyone else.
