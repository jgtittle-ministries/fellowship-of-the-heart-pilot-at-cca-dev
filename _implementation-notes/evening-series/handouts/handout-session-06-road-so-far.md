# Take-Home Sheet — Session 6: The Road So Far

*One quarter, five evenings, one road. A one-page memory of where we have
walked, to help with tonight's questions when they come home with you.*

## Session 1 — Welcome to the Journey

The container established — Safe, Present, Clear, Intentional — and the map
opened: the Four Connects (Self, Others, God, Mission). **Went home:** the
two standing daily questions — morning, five minutes: "Lord, what are you
up to today, and what do you want me to notice?"; evening, one journal
line: "What did I notice today?" *(Still running, every week since.)*

## Session 2 — The Soil of Your Heart

The Heart Soil diagnostic from Mark 4 — beginning to connect with self.
**Went home:** three times in the week, journal where each kind of soil
showed up that day. No fixing. Just noticing.

## Sessions 3–4 — Telling Your Story, I & II

One question, one circle, everyone told — parents first, then the teens at
their own depth: the practice of being known, under the psalm of being
completely known (Psalm 139). **Went home:** Joint Footprints — each
parent-and-teen pair walking each other through the footprints question,
twenty minutes together.

## Session 5 — Knowing and Being Known

The four conditions of a container — Safe, Present, Clear, Intentional —
applied to your real friendships, and the friendship map drawn. **Went
home:** one friendship, one condition, practiced more intentionally than
usual; journal what happened.

## Session 6 — Safe and Brave Together *(tonight)*

Confession and restoration, age-appropriate: each of us naming one place
where we want to walk in greater honesty — the place, not the details —
and the circle speaking a blessing of restoration over it. **Going home:**
the Five-Minute Examen, each evening through the two weeks — thank Him for
one specific thing; notice one place you saw God moving; ask Him about one
place you walked in less honesty than you wanted. Just notice. No fixing.
