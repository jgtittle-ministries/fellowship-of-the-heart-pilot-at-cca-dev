*One box per day. A missed box is never a failure — it is an invitation to return.*

## 1. The homework practice — week one

The Personal Doubts Inventory, twenty minutes once: list 5–10 scriptures you say
you believe; rate your trust in each, 1–5; for the lowest two or three, write the
specific doubt and sit with it. No shame; just data. The Quarterly Pulse (H11.4)
goes home tonight: five minutes, honest; bring it to The Return.

## 2. The end-of-week-one check-in

At the end of week one, tell your partner how it's going. A text or a call
counts. You are not grading each other; you are expecting each other. **Done:** ☐

## 3. The homework practice — week two

Two journal questions: Is there something I heard and did not obey? What is at
risk for me to hear? Not sure it was God? Test it with the How Did You Know? card,
in company.

now), the one you'd really like to do. If it slips, begin again the next day. No
