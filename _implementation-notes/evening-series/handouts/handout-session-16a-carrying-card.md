# Evening 16 — How to Carry the Word

*For every family, at the table tonight and on the fridge after. Choose a passage, learn it together, say it every day. A parent goes first.*

## Choosing the passage

**From your own journey,** if it can be: a passage that spoke in your PROAPT weeks, at Heart Bible Study, or in Doing the Word. **Short:** two to four verses for the family. **One translation,** so everyone says the same words. **Every age carries what it can:** one verse, or one line of it, counts as fully as four.

## Five ways to carry it

**1.  Write it out by hand.** Slowly, once, in your journal. The hand helps the memory.

**2.  First letters only.** Write the first letter of each word: *I h s u y w i m h* for "I have stored up your word in my heart." Say it from the letters.

**3.  A phrase at a time, building.** Say the first phrase three times. Add the second and say both. Keep adding until you have the verse.

**4.  Aloud, with a gesture.** Say it out loud, together, with a simple movement for each line. The body remembers what the ear hears.

**5.  Tie it to a moment.** A meal, the drive, bedtime, waking: the moments Deuteronomy 6 names. Say it at the same moment every day.
