# Evening 11 — Doing the Word in Pairs

*For the pair, away from the screen. A parent with their own teen by default. The parent goes first.*

## The times

**The parent's sentence** and the two questions, about six minutes. **Switch at 7:54.** **The teen's sentence,** to 8:00. **Pray for each other,** 8:00–8:06. **Back in the circle at 8:06.** *The container holder keeps the clock; you don't need to watch it.*

## The sentence

**I heard** ________ (from my journal, at Evening ____).\
**By** ________ (a day in the next two weeks)\
**I will** ________ (one specific thing).

## The two questions

**1.  Is it small enough to do in two weeks?** If not, make it smaller together. Not "be more patient with my brother" but "on Thursday, when he takes my charger, I will ask instead of yelling."

**2.  Does it involve another person?** If it does (an apology, making something right, a hard conversation), the step is talked over with a parent or another adult before it is taken.

## The test, before the doing

Three of the *How Did You Know?* checks from Evening 7 matter most tonight:

- **Scripture first:** does it line up with what God has already said?
- **Peace:** is there peace, even if the step is hard? Does it sound like the Lord's conviction, or like accusation?
- **Company:** what does your partner, who knows you and knows God, see?

*Nothing that fails the checks gets done (1 John 4:1). Choose again, or say "I'm still listening."*

## Pray for each other

One short prayer each, for your partner's step by name: "Lord, help ____ to ____ by ____."

## If you get stuck

**Can't find one:** "I'm still listening" is honest. Choose one during the week and tell your check-in partner. **Too private:** say "I have one" to the room. It is never something a teen has to hide from their parents. **Too big:** keep the big thing, and find its first small step.

*If something heavy comes up, honor it briefly and tell a leader after the close; don't work it through in the pair.*
