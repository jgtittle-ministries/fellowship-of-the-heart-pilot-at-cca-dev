# Held for later: the protocol review, before Going Out

*Held at John's word, 27 October 2026. Not published. A future discussion with the room and the leading team.*

## When

Before Going Out, toward the end of the current series. It is a discussion for a room that has walked enough evenings to judge them. The levels of internalization probably come in around the same time, near the end; the room already holds their key sign (see below).

## Where it came from

At the dinner on 26 October, the room talked about how you can tell whether this is working. John added a thought about how the evenings were chosen. Choosing them was a trade-off between two aims:

- **Effective:** progressive internalization.
- **Efficient:** the smallest set of evenings that brings it about.

A session is not final just because we did it. The choice can be questioned, and the room can help question it.

The same evening, John offered one measure of internalization: when life interrupts the practice, do you return to it? This is the sign the September design named as the move from Level 2 to Level 3: returns, not streaks. A streak measures compliance; coming back unprompted measures wanting it.

## The discussion

**Three questions.** How do you tell? How do you choose? What did we decide?

**The protocols to review.** Not only this series, but earlier ones as well:

- the evenings of this series, one by one;
- Spiritual Styles;
- "Who Are You?";
- the Four Connects;
- and others used along the way.

**Three sorts.** For each protocol, which parts are:

1. **The same, always:** the core that makes it work. Change it, and it is no longer the practice.
2. **Adaptable, by design:** what may change to fit the room, the size, the age or the time.
3. **In the moment:** not part of the protocol at all, but how a leader adapts, manages and facilitates on the night. Leading when the evening runs late is one example (Evening 6).

## Open when it is taken up

- Which evening holds it, or whether it is a planning-lab discussion with the leading team first.
- Whether the teens sort the protocols themselves, with the adults in the circle.
- How the levels of internalization are introduced alongside it.
- Where the results go: the Evening Handbook, the leader notes, and the action research article (its findings are sorted the same way: held, adapted, changed).
- A changelog entry when anything changes because of it.
