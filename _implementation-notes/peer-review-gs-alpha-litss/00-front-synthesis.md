## What this review means for Getting Started

The structure held. Twenty-one adversarially verified findings produced zero refutations and no challenge to the container, the family-circle design, the teen-leadership architecture, or the safety apparatus — the parts of the design that carry the most risk drew the panel's strongest praise, and six-per-reviewer suspicions died against text that had already answered them. What the review exposes is a perimeter question, not a structural one: everything inside the room is engineered with rare care, and every joint between the room and what lies outside it — the unconvinced family at the door, the gospel not yet believed, the Spirit's personal work, the churches the families go home to, the next cohort — is either high, missing, or silent. The two traditions empaneled precisely because they own that perimeter said so in unison, while both conceding the interior is stronger than their own instruments. The mechanical Tier 1 items should be swept before September regardless; the Tier 2 questions are the founder's to adjudicate — they are not editorial notes but genuine design decisions about what this instrument is for, and the panel's gift is that it has made the cost of each answer visible.
